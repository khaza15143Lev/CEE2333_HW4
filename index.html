Problem 3. Maximum score = 25 | Total score = 0.0

A rigid-jointed 2D frame has one free joint loaded by a point force and a moment; every other joint is a fully fixed support (fixed against x-translation, y-translation, and rotation). Each member is a frame (beam-column) element with both axial and bending stiffness. Work through the FEM steps below; for each step, you can either type your answers directly or upload a CSV file in the format shown in that step's example file, using the step's Import button.

Each member rigidly connects one fixed support to the free joint. EA is the member's axial rigidity (Young's modulus × area) and EI is its bending rigidity (Young's modulus × moment of inertia).
